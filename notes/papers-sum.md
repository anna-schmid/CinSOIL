# Summary of Papers

- [Batjes et al. (2022)](../papers/Batjes-2024.pdf): Providing quality-assessed and standardised soil data to support global mapping and modelling
(WoSIS snapshot 2023)


- [Brøeg et al. (2024)](../papers/Broeg-2024.pdf): Spatiotemporal Monitoring of Cropland Soil Organic Carbon Changes From Space


- [Cornu et al. (2023)](../papers/Cornu-2023.pdf): National soil data in EU countries, where do we stand?

Survey in Spring 2020 in the frame of the European Joint Porgramme on agirultural soils (EJP) launched by the European Commission, 170 national/regional soil datasets across 24 European countries. Survey will become a contribution to the European Soil Overvatory, launched in December 2020.
Most cover core data like SOC, pH, texture, but biological data, water content, and organic pollutants are rare. Datasets vary in time span, methods, and accessibility.
Biggest need: harmonization and minimum standards to enable EU-wide soil health monitoring.

- [Yuzugullu et al. (2024)](../papers/Yuzugullu-2024.pdf): Satellite-based soil organic carbon mapping on European soils using available datasets and support sampling



- [Vadour et al. (2022)](../papers/Vadour-2024.pdf): Satellite Imagery to Map Topsoil Organic Carbon Content over Cultivated Areas: An Overview

Review paper is dedicated to the satellite-based spectral approaches for SOC assessment that have been achieved from several satellite sensors, study scales and geographical contexts in the past decade.
